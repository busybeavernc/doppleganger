Installing on Windows
  1. Open DopplegangerSetup.exe. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway.
  2. The setup guide adds the Doppleganger camera, helps you pick your webcam and record your first loop.
  3. In Zoom or Teams, choose Doppleganger as your camera.
Installing on a Mac
  1. Open the .dmg and drag Doppleganger onto Applications.
  2. The first time, right-click Doppleganger → Open. (macOS 15+: if there's no Open button, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.)
  3. Install OBS Studio (free). Doppleganger uses its virtual camera, and the setup guide walks you through the one-time steps.
  4. In Zoom, Teams or Meet, choose OBS Virtual Camera.
